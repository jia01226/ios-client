## Garden94Atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent sprite atlas for a restrained French moonlight camellia exploration game, same soft hand-painted animated-film background aesthetic, muted Morandi blue-grey and dusty pink, all sprites ONE coherent painterly style and ONE upper-right soft silvery light source. Not photorealistic, not plastic, no sharp sticker outlines, no white halos. Square 2048x2048 image, exact 4 columns by 4 rows of equally sized invisible cells, generous transparent padding in every cell, no grid lines, no labels, no text. Each sprite centered fully inside its cell. Row 1: four different camellia shrubs, no flowers and no buds, open natural foliage silhouettes with exposed branching tips suitable for attaching individual memory flowers; bases fade organically into transparency. Row 2: three distinct foreground leafy camellia branches in cells 1-3 and one soft small fallen camellia petal in cell 4. Row 3: one pale pink frontal camellia bloom, one ivory three-quarter camellia bloom, one muted dusty burgundy half-open camellia, one closed pink bud; only the flower head with tiny green sepal, natural softly painted edges. Row 4: four successive animation frames of the SAME small ethereal translucent mother-of-pearl butterfly centered at same position and body size, wings open then half closed then fully closed then half open; misty blue-white gossamer wings, delicate not neon, no trail painted in. Exact regular 4x4 layout for automatic texture slicing. All greenery subdued blue-green grey only. Real alpha background everywhere outside objects.

## Garden94Landscape

Use case: stylized-concept. Create the far background plate for the SAME SpriteKit camellia exploration game as the supplied atlas (style reference only). Wide landscape 3:2 composition, soft hand-painted animated-film gouache, subdued Morandi blue-grey, pale grey lavender, silver mist. A continuous spacious French woodland glade under a small pale moon in the upper right, distant hazy rounded mountains and soft silhouettes of small trees, lower half is softly painted blue grey mist and earth, an indistinct winding pale path. Same upper-right diffused light source and painterly grain as atlas. Calm, romantic but restrained. Keep broad quiet low-detail ground for runtime shrubs; NO flowers, NO foreground plants, NO sharp near objects, NO architecture, NO text, NO UI. Edges of hills dissolve into atmosphere; no spotlight or dramatic sunbeams. Paint at luminous twilight exposure, usable as a base for day tint and darker night tint without clipped highlights. One entire landscape, not collage, not atlas. Horizon about upper third, soft ground occupies bottom two thirds.
